## Full spotlight text (extracted)

Miles says what he enjoys most about working at Gursey is the opportunity to learn more about Business Management and Accounting while collaborating with a supportive team. He values working closely with both his teammates and clients and enjoys being part of an environment where there's always something new to learn.

Some accomplishments Miles is especially proud of include automating several internal projects, improving credit card coding and brokerage accounting processes, and leveraging AI to develop smarter, more efficient workflows. He enjoys finding ways to streamline everyday tasks and is always looking for opportunities to improve existing processes.

Miles is motivated by working alongside his team and building strong relationships with clients. His advice to his colleagues is to always understand the "why" behind a process rather than simply repeating the task. By thinking outside the box and focusing on the bigger picture, he believes teams can uncover smarter, more efficient ways of working.

Many coworkers may not know that Miles played lacrosse in high school, has traveled to Guatemala more than 20 times, has six siblings, and studied abroad in Melbourne, Australia. He describes Melbourne as having the perfect blend of Los Angeles and New York. He's also a big fan of the movie Nacho Libre and is the proud owner of both a cat and a gecko.

Outside of work, Miles enjoys spending time with friends and family and staying active through surfing, snowboarding, golf, tennis, bowling, fishing, and camping. He's also an avid gamer and lives by the Golden Rule: "Treat others the way you want to be treated."

Fun Facts: Miles is a night owl and a devoted coffee drinker whose favorite cold brew comes from Neighborhood on Beverly Grove. His go-to lunch spot is Benihana, and his dream vacation includes exploring both Hawaii and Japan.
