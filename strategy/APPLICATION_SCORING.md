# Application scoring rubric (1–5)

Score **before** applying. Only apply if **total ≥ 16** (avg 4+) or any single dimension = 5 with none below 3.

## Dimensions (1–5 each)

### 1. Title & JD fit

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Product Ops, BizOps, Implementation, Business Systems, Onboarding (SaaS), RevOps with systems focus |
| 4 | Operations Analyst with automation/AI in JD |
| 3 | Generic “Operations” — read full JD |
| 2 | Mixed ops + heavy accounting |
| 1 | Coordinator, AP/AR, CS, SDR, help desk |

### 2. Boredom / creativity

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Build workflows, tools, cross-functional systems |
| 4 | Process improvement + some build |
| 3 | Mix admin + projects |
| 2 | Mostly reporting/scheduling |
| 1 | Repetitive data entry |

### 3. Culture & location

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | **US remote** |
| 4 | **LA / Santa Monica hybrid**, 1–2 days in-office max |
| 3 | Unclear RTO (treat as skip until clarified) |
| 2 | Hybrid outside LA (SF, NYC, etc.) or 3+ days office |
| 1 | Toxic signals / call center pace / 5-day RTO |

### 4. Comp & level

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Posted $110k+ or “$100k–$130k” |
| 4 | $90k–$109k at strong company |
| 3 | No salary, role fits |
| 2 | Under $90k |
| 1 | Intern / trainee / “competitive” at coordinator level |

### 5. Win probability

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | 2–4 yr req, automation keywords match your projects |
| 4 | Stretch but portfolio/GitHub backs it |
| 3 | Neutral |
| 2 | Requires 6+ yrs or Salesforce expert you don’t have |
| 1 | 50+ applicants, niche credential |

## Quick reject (any = skip)

- [ ] “Accounting degree required” / CPA track
- [ ] 5 days in-office LA traffic hell
- [ ] Hybrid or onsite outside LA / Santa Monica (SF 3x/week, NYC, Foster City, etc.)
- [ ] "Remote" job that requires regular non-LA office days
- [ ] Pure customer support / ticket queue
- [ ] Commission-only or SDR

## Record in tracker

`tracking/applications.csv` — columns match scoring + status.
